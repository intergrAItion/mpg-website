*Look beyond the headline fee and understand the service, separate costs and support your rental needs.*

A management fee is easier to assess when you can see what it covers. The property, the work involved and the support you need all matter. A useful comparison starts with the complete quote and a clear picture of the everyday service.

## Look at the complete quote

Ask about the ongoing management fee and any separate tenant placement, onboarding, renewal, inspection or administration charges. These are questions to ask, rather than a list of charges every manager applies. Find out which costs are recurring, which arise only when a particular service is needed, and whether the quoted amounts include any applicable tax.

Check how the fee is calculated: what amount it applies to, when it becomes payable and what happens during a vacant period. Comparing the same scope and assumptions helps you understand the costs more clearly than looking at a percentage in isolation.

## Consider the everyday support

Tenant communication, maintenance coordination, lease administration, inspections and financial reporting are part of the work that continues after a tenant moves in. Think about which details you want to take off your hands, and which decisions you want to retain.

A useful service description explains who follows up on an outstanding matter, how a maintenance decision reaches you and where the tenancy records are kept. Technology can make administration more convenient, but the useful question is how the service helps you keep a clear view of your rental.

## Ask for a reporting example

Ask your prospective manager to walk you through an anonymised example of a statement or property update. Can you follow the payment entries and separate costs? Is it clear which maintenance matters remain outstanding? A readable report gives you a better basis for a conversation about your property.

## Questions to take to your next conversation

- What is included in the ongoing fee, and what is charged separately?
- How will I receive property updates and raise a question?
- Which maintenance decisions need my approval?
- What reporting can I expect, and how are outstanding matters followed up?
- What are the appointment and handover arrangements?
